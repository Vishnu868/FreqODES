FreqODES adds no layers: its score works on the raw B×256×256 slices. The ODES BN-KL score uses the batch statistics of the last 9 BN layers. Slice diversity (farthest-first) uses the layer4 features pooled to 512-d; the patch diversity map uses the 64×64×64 layer1 features.
